Privacy Policy for Apply.X

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Apply.X ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI-powered career companion designed to help users with career planning, job preparation, resume support, skill development and related career activities.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Apply.X, we may collect information such as your email address, account information, career preferences, job-related information, resume-related information and information that you choose to provide while using the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide and improve Apply.X features, authenticate accounts, personalize career guidance, provide AI-powered recommendations, analyze job-related information and maintain the functionality and security of the service.

3. AI Processing

Some information submitted to Apply.X may be processed by third-party AI service providers in order to provide features such as career guidance, job analysis and personalized recommendations.

4. Account Authentication

Apply.X uses authentication services to securely manage user accounts. Your login credentials are handled through the authentication system and are not intentionally stored as plain-text passwords by Apply.X.

5. Data Storage

Information may be stored using third-party cloud database and authentication services used by Apply.X. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate Apply.X, such as authentication, database, hosting and AI service providers.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us through the support contact provided with the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Apply.X is intended for users who are legally permitted to use career and employment-related services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the support email provided on the Apply.X app listing.

12. Acceptance
